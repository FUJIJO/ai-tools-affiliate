# Gamma vs MiriCanvas: Which Free AI Slide Maker Fits a Busy Side Hustle? (2026)

I'm Takeru, a self-confessed lazy dad who runs a money-and-productivity blog on the side. Between work decks, school-event handouts, and slides explaining my own side-hustle services, I make more presentations than I'd like. And I'm bad at design. A blank slide can eat 30 minutes before I've typed a word.

So I compared two AI slide makers that let you type a topic (or paste a draft) and get a structured, designed deck back: **Gamma** and **MiriCanvas**. Both have free plans, so this comparison focuses on one question: **how far can you get for free, and what happens when you outgrow it?**

To be upfront: this is a comparison of what the official pricing pages and help centers said on November 17, 2026 — not a long-term hands-on review. AI-generated slides are drafts, and you still need to check the content. And no tool guarantees side-hustle income.

## Quick verdict

- **You also make social graphics, flyers, or thumbnails** → MiriCanvas. The free plan includes 5 AI Presentation generations per month, and the tool doubles as a general design app.
- **You want long decks or web-page-style docs from one prompt, and free PPTX export** → Gamma. You get 400 credits at signup and can export to PPTX on the free plan.
- **Not sure?** Both are free to start. Make one real deck in each and keep the one whose output you like.

## They count usage differently

This is the part that trips people up.

- **Gamma uses credits.** Generating slides and images, editing with the AI agent, and trying new layouts all draw credits, and Gamma's help center says the amount varies by task, model, and length. There's no fixed "credits per deck" number.
- **MiriCanvas counts generations.** Free users get 5 AI Presentations a month; Pro is listed as unlimited.

My own volume is about 2–4 decks a month across work and the blog, so MiriCanvas's free tier would likely cover me. If you build a deck every week, compare the paid tiers instead.

## Gamma: big decks, generous free exports

According to Gamma's help center:

- **Free:** 400 credits at signup that don't refill; up to 10 slides per prompt; 200 bonus credits per referral (up to 2,000 credits held at once).
- **Plus:** 1,500 monthly credits; up to 100 slides per prompt; removes the "Made with Gamma" badge.
- **Pro:** 4,000 monthly credits; API access; publish up to 10 sites on custom domains; detailed analytics.
- **Ultra:** 20,000 monthly credits (monthly billing only for now).
- Billing is per user, and unused plan credits roll over up to 2× your monthly allowance.

**Pros**

- No credit card needed to start, and free exports to PPTX and Google Slides.
- Good for turning a long draft into a big deck in one go.

**Cons**

- Free credits don't refill, so heavy testing burns through them.
- Credit usage per deck is unpredictable.
- I couldn't retrieve current dollar prices from the official pricing page, so check it before upgrading.

## MiriCanvas: the cheap all-rounder

MiriCanvas is a browser design tool (presentations, social posts, posters, and more). Its pricing page lists 50,000+ templates, 350,000+ photos and graphics, and 1GB of storage on the free plan. Pro adds unlimited premium templates and assets, 10GB, background removal, AI tools, and 7-day version history.

Per its (Japanese) help center, AI Presentation accepts up to 20,000 characters of text, file uploads (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, etc.) are Pro-only, and input data is not used to train AI models. Downloads include JPG, PNG, PDF, PPTX, MP4, and GIF; in PPTX files, text stays editable but other elements are converted to images.

**Pros**

- 5 free AI decks a month, plus a full design tool for everything else.
- Pro is inexpensive (US$129.99/year as shown to me).
- Explicit statement that your inputs aren't used for AI training.

**Cons**

- The first-month free Pro trial auto-renews unless you cancel; already-paid periods aren't refunded.
- Prices appear to vary by region and currency — confirm at checkout.
- If you need to fine-tune in PowerPoint, test the PPTX export first (non-text elements become images).

## Using AI slides for a side hustle

My favorite use: pasting a blog post into the generator to get a visual summary I can reuse as social images or a downloadable PDF. Just remember that AI can get facts and numbers wrong — verify them. If you plan to sell or distribute anything built with templates, read each tool's license first. ## FAQ

**Can I get by on the free plans?**
For a few decks a month, probably. Gamma gives 400 one-time credits; MiriCanvas gives 5 AI Presentations per month.

**Can I edit the output in PowerPoint?**
Both export PPTX. Gamma lists PPTX export on its free plan. MiriCanvas keeps text editable but converts other elements to images.

**Will this make me money?**
Not by itself. It can cut the time you spend on slides; what you do with that time is up to you.
