### How far do credits go?

On the website, text-to-speech costs **1 credit per character**. So the free plan is roughly 10,000 characters a month, and Creator roughly 121,000. A 1,000-word blog post is around 5,000–6,000 characters in English, so the free plan covers maybe one or two posts; Creator covers a lot more.

But credits are shared across products: **speech-to-text uses 330 credits per minute, music about 900 per minute, and dubbing 2,000+ per minute.** Branch out beyond narration and your balance drops fast.

Two details lazy users will appreciate—and one to watch:

- Unused credits on paid plans **roll over for up to two months** (capped).
- **Downgrading or cancelling ends rollover**, and leftover paid credits are lost once the cycle ends.
