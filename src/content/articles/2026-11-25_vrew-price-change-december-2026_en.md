# Vrew Price Change (Dec 1, 2026): Should You Subscribe Before November 30?

If you make YouTube clips, Shorts, or side-hustle videos, there's a good chance Vrew does your captions and text-based cuts. Vrew has announced that **new plans and a new refund policy start on December 1, 2026**—and the one question that matters for a busy creator is simple: *do I need to do anything before then?*

Short version: it depends almost entirely on **how many credits you actually use** and **whether you plan to keep your current plan unchanged**.

A quick note on scope: this is a summary of Vrew's and OpusClip's official help and pricing pages as checked on **November 25, 2026 (JST)**, not a long-term hands-on review of the new plans. Prices below are the **Japanese yen prices from Vrew's Japan announcement**; prices in other regions and currencies differ. And no tool guarantees you'll earn money from clips.

## The 30-second answer

- **You use about 2,000 credits a month and will stay on Light** → Locking in now saves a little (monthly Light goes from ¥1,590 to ¥1,900).
- **You burn through Standard's 10,000 credits every month** → This is where staying on your current plan matters most: the equivalent new Standard 10K is ¥4,300/month vs. ¥2,490 today.
- **You're on Standard but use under 5,000 credits** → No rush. The new Standard 5K (¥2,600/month, ¥21,600/year) may actually suit you better.
- **You're still on the free plan** → Check whether you even run out of the 200 free monthly credits before buying an annual plan in a panic.

The key line from Vrew: **if you don't cancel or change your current paid plan, your price, credits, and refund policy stay the same**, and your subscription won't switch to a new plan automatically at renewal. No action is needed to keep it.

**How far do credits go?** Per Vrew's announcement, speech analysis (transcription) costs 1 credit per minute, and AI voice costs 1 credit per 100 characters. The free plan's 200 credits is roughly 200 minutes of transcription a month. Unused credits don't roll over.

## What else changes (the good and the gotchas)

**Good news**
- **Cheaper add-on credits for every paid plan, including Light.** The old "Usage History Eraser" (¥800 per 1,000 credits, Standard and up only) is replaced by additional credits at ¥660 per 1,000, with bonus credits of up to 27.5% on bulk purchases. Unused add-on credits can be refunded (bonus credits excluded). Note: the Japanese notice says they're valid for 6 months from purchase, while the US notice says 1 year—check your region's terms.
- **Self-serve downgrades.** Scale up in a busy month, scale down when life gets in the way.
- **No refund fee within 14 days** under the new policy, even if you've used credits; a full refund if you haven't.

**Gotchas**
- **Changing plans on or after Dec 1 moves you to new pricing and policy** from that day. If you want to keep the old price, don't casually switch.
- Refunds on new or changed plans will factor in **credits you've used**.
- The US notice also introduces a **one-account-per-person policy** from December 1. I didn't find the same wording in the Japanese notice today, so check yours if you juggle several free accounts.

## Pre-purchase checklist

1. Look at your last 2–3 months of credit usage inside Vrew.
2. Be honest: will you keep this exact plan without changing it?
3. Only buy annual if you'll actually make videos all year.
4. Before paying a large amount, ask Vrew's in-app chat whether a purchase made by November 30 keeps today's price at renewal. The Japanese notice says plans bought by Nov 30 keep the current refund policy even after auto-renewal, which suggests they're treated as current plans—but a one-line confirmation is cheap insurance.

## FAQ

No. Vrew says your price, credits, and refund policy stay the same as long as you don't cancel or change plans.

**Will my plan switch automatically on December 1?**
No. According to Vrew's US FAQ, renewals don't switch you to a new plan.

**When can I choose a new plan?**
From December 1, 2026—7:00 a.m. KST per the US notice (the same clock time in Japan).

The December announcement doesn't mention changes to the free plan (200 credits/month, per Vrew's April 2026 credit-unification notice).
