# Best AI Writing Tools for a Side-Hustle Blog in 2026

This guide compares a general-purpose assistant (ChatGPT) with two Japan-market writing tools that are useful if you publish in Japanese: Value AI Writer and Catchy. - Start with ChatGPT’s free plan if you need a flexible drafting and editing partner.

## How to choose without overspending

1. If you only need one draft this week, stay on a free plan.
2. If you publish Japanese blog posts on a regular cadence, consider Value AI Writer Entry (JPY 840/month for 3 articles) after you have tried the free tier.
3. If most of your work is captions or ads, use Catchy’s free credits first.

Paying for three tools at once makes it harder to recoup the cost. A subscription does not create a side income by itself.

## FAQ

### Can I publish AI output as-is?

You should not. Value AI Writer’s official FAQ says generated text is not always accurate and should be reviewed. Check facts, figures, quotes, and third-party rights yourself.

### Can I run a side hustle on free plans only?

You can use free plans to practice drafting. Free tiers have limits. Upgrading does not guarantee that you will earn money.

### Which one tool should I pick?

If your work is Japanese SEO posts, Value AI Writer is the more specialized option. If you still do not know what you will write, start with ChatGPT. If you need short Japanese promotional copy, start with Catchy. Try the free output first.
