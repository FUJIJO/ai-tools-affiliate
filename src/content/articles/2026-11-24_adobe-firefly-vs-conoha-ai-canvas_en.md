AI image generators fix that. But once you start comparing them, the real headache is **billing**: monthly credits, daily free limits, pay-per-minute GPU time. For a lazy person, the risk isn't ugly images. It's **paying for something you don't fully understand and getting a bigger bill than expected.**

## The real difference: credits vs. the meter

**Firefly works like a prepaid phone plan.** You pay a set amount, get a bucket of credits, and when the bucket is empty you either wait, upgrade, or buy more. Adobe says credits reset on your billing date and don't roll over. So the risk is paying for credits you never use in a busy month, not a surprise bill.

**ConoHa AI Canvas works like a taxi meter with some free kilometers.** You pay a monthly base fee, and the meter runs only while the WebUI is running. Go past your free hours and it's ¥6.6 per minute (¥396 an hour). Forgetting to stop it for an extra five hours on the Entry plan would add ¥1,980 to the ¥1,100 base fee.

ConoHa does have guardrails: an **auto-shutdown timer** that stops the WebUI after a period of inactivity, and you can change the timer yourself. Two more things in its official FAQ are worth knowing:

- If the WebUI runs across a month boundary, **all of that time counts toward the month it ended**, and last month's leftover free hours don't carry over.
- Plans **auto-renew monthly**. Cancelling is a "cancellation reservation" that takes effect at the **end of the current month**. You can't cancel immediately.

## Pros and cons

## A lazy person's three rules

1. **On the day you subscribe, put the review date in your calendar**: the end of Adobe's sale period, or ConoHa's month-end cancellation.
2. **With anything metered, set the auto-shutdown timer short on day one.**
3. **Don't generate real logos, famous characters, or celebrity look-alikes.** Every service here puts responsibility for how you use the output on you.

## FAQ

**Can I generate AI images completely free?**
Yes, with limits. Firefly Free, plus the free plans of ChatGPT and Canva, all allowed some image generation as of November 24, 2026. Limits change, so check before you rely on them.

**Can I use AI images on a monetized blog?**
Often yes, but the terms differ. Adobe says content from Firefly's own models is safe for commercial use. ConoHa AI Canvas's terms allow commercial use, but you also need to follow the license of the model you pick.

**Does ConoHa AI Canvas charge when I'm not generating?**
The per-minute fee only runs while the WebUI is running, not while you're just in the control panel. The monthly base fee always applies.

**Can I sign up for ConoHa AI Canvas now?**
Not as of November 24, 2026. The official site says new sign-ups are suspended for maintenance. Check the official news page for the restart.

**Will Adobe charge me more if I run out of credits?**
Adobe's FAQ says no. Paid plans keep standard generation unlimited after credits run out, and there's no extra charge unless you buy more credits.

Start free and count how many images you actually make in a month. That number decides it. If you want a predictable bill, go with Firefly. If you'll make lots of images in your own style and can keep an eye on the meter, ConoHa AI Canvas is worth a look once sign-ups reopen.
